Privacy Policy

Wireless Mouse & Keyboard · by Skills BD Academy · Effective date: November 20, 2026

Wireless Mouse & Keyboard (“the app”) turns your Android phone into a mouse, keyboard and remote control for your own Windows PC. It works together with a free Windows program (“the PC server”) that you install on your computer. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Summary

We do not have user accounts. We do not run any server that receives your data. We do not sell your data. Everything the app sends goes directly between your phone and your own PC, over your Wi‑Fi network or a USB cable.

What the app handles

  • Input you create on the phone: touch movements, clicks, scrolls, typed text, key presses and shortcuts. These are sent to your PC so it can act on them.
  • Clipboard text and files you choose to send: when you use the clipboard or file transfer features, the content goes to your PC (or from your PC to your phone). Files received on the PC are saved in the Downloads\PhoneMouse folder.
  • PC screen images: if you use the screen view feature, pictures of your PC screen are sent to your phone and shown live. They are not saved by the app.
  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing QR code shown by the PC server. No photo or video is stored or sent.
  • Microphone and voice typing: used only when you tap the voice typing button. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service built into your Android device, which is usually provided by Google. Audio may therefore be processed by that service under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your voice.
  • Network address and pairing key: the PC’s IP address and a secret pairing key are stored on your phone so you can reconnect. The key is created on your PC and never leaves your devices.
  • App PIN (optional): if you turn on the PIN lock, only a salted hash of the PIN is stored on your phone. It is never sent anywhere.

Security

Messages between the phone and the PC are encrypted with AES‑GCM using the pairing key, and include protection against replay of old messages. Only a phone that has the pairing key can control your PC. Keep the QR code and key private. Anyone who has them while on the same network could control your PC.

Data we collect and share

We, the developer, do not collect, receive, store or share your personal data. The app does not include analytics or crash‑reporting tools at the time of this policy.

Advertising

The app does not currently show advertisements. If we add ads in a future version, such ads may be provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID and show personalized or non‑personalized ads based on your choices. Where required, we will ask for your consent first, and we will update this policy before that change is released.

Where data is stored and how to delete it

Settings, the pairing key and the optional PIN hash are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. The PC server stores its pairing key and settings on your PC in the PhoneMouse folder inside your Windows user data folder (%APPDATA%). You can delete that folder at any time. Files you transferred stay in your Downloads\PhoneMouse folder until you delete them.

Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s store listing or update notes.
